# Tell Me About a Time You Influenced Without Authority

**Story:** To choose

## What the interviewer is listening for

- You build alignment across teams without relying on title or escalation.
- You understand other teams’ incentives, constraints, and concerns.
- You use evidence and practical collaboration to move work forward.

## Skim notes

- Choose cross-team dependency.
- Understand their concerns.
- Listen, adapt, build.
- Use concrete mechanism.
- Preserve relationship afterward.

## Details to add before the interview

- What did you need from the other team and why?
- What were their valid concerns or competing priorities?
- How did you change your approach based on their input?
- What evidence moved the decision forward?

## Likely follow-ups

- What did you do when someone still disagreed?
- Why did you not escalate sooner?
- How did you ensure the outcome would last?
