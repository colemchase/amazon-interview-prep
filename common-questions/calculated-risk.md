# Tell Me About a Calculated Risk You Took

**Story:** To choose

## What the interviewer is listening for

- You can move forward with incomplete information without being reckless.
- You explicitly assess downside, reversibility, and customer impact.
- You learn from the outcome whether it succeeds or not.

## Skim notes

- Inaction has cost.
- Assess risk evidence.
- Establish safety guardrails.
- Decide, then measure.
- Capture risk lessons.

## Details to add before the interview

- What was uncertain and why could you not wait for perfect information?
- What was the worst credible downside?
- Which safeguards made the risk acceptable?
- What data or feedback caused you to continue, stop, or adjust?

## Likely follow-ups

- What would have made you choose not to take the risk?
- How did you communicate the tradeoff?
- Was the risk worth it in hindsight?
