# Tell Me About a Time You Received Feedback

**Story:** To choose

## What the interviewer is listening for

- You seek and accept candid feedback without becoming defensive.
- You turn feedback into a specific behavior change.
- You can show evidence that the change worked.

## Skim notes

- Choose meaningful feedback, not a disguised compliment.
- State the feedback plainly and explain why it was fair or useful.
- Describe your initial response briefly, then focus on what you changed.
- Name the concrete new behavior, process, or habit.
- Close with a measurable or observed improvement.

## Details to add before the interview

- Who gave the feedback and in what context?
- What exact behavior or impact did they point out?
- What did you change immediately and over time?
- How did you ask for follow-up feedback or measure improvement?
- What did you learn about yourself?

## Likely follow-ups

- What feedback do you still find difficult to hear?
- How do you ask for feedback proactively?
- How did you know the change had stuck?
