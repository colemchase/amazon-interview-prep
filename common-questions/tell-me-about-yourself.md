# Tell Me About Yourself

## Answer structure

> I am a platform and SRE-focused engineer who enjoys making cloud systems easier and safer for the developers who use them. My recent work has centered on AWS, EKS, CI/CD, and operational automation. For example, I led a CI/CD migration that gave development teams clearer deployment visibility and was adopted across hundreds of services. I have also standardized node access across environments for a successful FedRAMP audit and automated recurring maintenance work so our service desk backlog was cleared for the first time in over a year. The common thread is that I like finding the operational constraint behind a problem, fixing it durably, and creating capacity for teams to move faster. I am looking for a role where I can apply that platform experience at larger scale while continuing to deepen my AWS and Kubernetes expertise.

## Skim notes

- Platform SRE engineer.
- Migrate hundreds services.
- Enable FedRAMP access.
- Clear ticket backlog.
- Remove recurring constraints.
- Scale platform impact.

## Why this answer works

- It gives a concise professional theme instead of a chronological resume walkthrough.
- It supports the theme with three distinct, credible outcomes.
- It ends with a forward-looking reason for the role.

## Details to confirm

- Your current title, employer, total years of experience, and most relevant earlier role.
- The specific Amazon/AWS role or team, so the final sentence can be tailored.
- Any credential or degree worth mentioning only if it strengthens the target role.

## Likely follow-ups

- Which of those projects had the greatest customer impact?
- What type of platform problem do you most enjoy solving?
- Why Amazon or AWS, and why this role now?
