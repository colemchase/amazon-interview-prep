# Tell Me About a Decision You Made Using Data

**Story:** To choose

## What the interviewer is listening for

- You use evidence to make a better decision, not to decorate a decision already made.
- You choose the data that matters and recognize its limitations.
- You can combine metrics with technical judgment and customer context.

## Skim notes

- Intuition insufficient alone.
- Frame decision question.
- Measure relevant signals.
- Translate insight action.
- Validate limitations explicitly.

## Details to add before the interview

- What data sources, baseline, or comparison did you use?
- How did you validate accuracy or avoid misleading conclusions?
- What decision would you have made without the data?
- Which metric changed after your decision?

## Likely follow-ups

- What did the data not tell you?
- Did anyone challenge your interpretation?
- How did you know correlation was not being mistaken for causation?
