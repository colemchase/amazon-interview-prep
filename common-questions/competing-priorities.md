# Tell Me About a Time You Had Competing Priorities

**Story:** To choose

## What the interviewer is listening for

- You make tradeoffs based on customer impact and business priorities.
- You communicate what will not happen, not only what will.
- You protect quality while delivering the highest-value work.

## Skim notes

- Pick a moment when two important commitments conflicted.
- State how you ranked them: customer impact, risk, deadline, or reversibility.
- Explain what you deferred, delegated, reduced, or renegotiated.
- Show how you communicated the tradeoff early.
- Close with the outcome and what you learned about prioritization.

## Details to add before the interview

- What made each priority legitimate?
- What criteria did you use to choose?
- Who needed to agree or be informed?
- What was deferred and how did you make sure it was revisited?

## Likely follow-ups

- What would you do if a stakeholder rejected your prioritization?
- Did any customer experience a negative impact?
- How did you know you made the right tradeoff?
