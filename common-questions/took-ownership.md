# Tell Me About a Time You Took Ownership

**Leadership Principle:** Ownership  
**Use:** [Automating SRE Maintenance](../core-stories/sre-maintenance-automation.md)

## Draft answer

> After joining an SRE team, recurring maintenance fixes consumed so much time that developer requests and service desk work kept accumulating. I could have continued closing maintenance tasks one at a time, but I saw that the operating model itself was the problem. I identified the work that required no ongoing judgment and automated it. For tasks that needed light supervision, I created reusable workflows so they could run in the background rather than monopolizing active SRE time. I also kept the work needing human judgment manual, with the appropriate oversight. This freed capacity for developer requests and other tickets, and we cleared the service desk board for the first time in more than a year. The important ownership lesson was to remove the recurring constraint, not merely work harder inside it.
