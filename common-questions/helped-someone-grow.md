# Tell Me About a Time You Helped Someone Grow

**Story:** To choose

## What the interviewer is listening for

- You invest in other people’s development, even without formal management responsibility.
- You tailor support to what the person needs instead of simply giving answers.
- Your help creates lasting capability and independence.

## Skim notes

- Choose growth opportunity.
- Identify development gap.
- Coach, review, practice.
- Build independence gradually.
- Credit their growth.

## Details to add before the interview

- What did the person want or need to learn?
- How did you diagnose the gap and agree on a plan?
- What support did you provide, and what did you deliberately let them own?
- What changed for them, the team, or customers?

## Likely follow-ups

- How did you know your coaching was working?
- What did you do when they struggled?
- How do you make time for developing others alongside delivery work?
