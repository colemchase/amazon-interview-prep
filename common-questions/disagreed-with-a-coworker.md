# Tell Me About a Time You Disagreed with a Coworker

**Story:** To choose

## What the interviewer is listening for

- You address disagreement directly and respectfully.
- You use data, customer impact, or shared goals—not ego—to make your case.
- You can commit once a decision is made, even if your preferred approach is not chosen.

## Skim notes

- Choose real disagreement.
- Start shared goal.
- Bring supporting evidence.
- Explain decision aftermath.
- Share relationship outcome.

## Details to add before the interview

- Who was involved and what was the decision?
- Why was the other person’s position reasonable?
- What evidence, experiment, or customer need informed your view?
- Was your approach selected? If not, how did you commit?
- What changed in the relationship or team process afterward?

## Likely follow-ups

- What would you do differently next time?
- How did you make sure the disagreement did not become personal?
- What did you do after the decision was made?
