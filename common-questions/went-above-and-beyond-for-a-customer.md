# Tell Me About a Time You Went Above and Beyond for a Customer

**Leadership Principle:** Customer Obsession

**Use:** [Making CI/CD Easier for Developers](../core-stories/cicd-pipeline-migration.md)

## Draft answer

> Developers were struggling with a legacy CI/CD pipeline that was difficult to understand and gave them little visibility into what had been deployed to each environment. Rather than design a solution only from the platform side, I spent my own time and resources building a representative environment at home. I created sample developer projects, practiced the workflow myself, and evaluated the tools from the same perspective as the teams we supported. That helped me choose the right approach, design a clearer CI/CD workflow, and plan the migration from the legacy system. I then delivered the migration in production. The result was clearer promotion validation and visibility into the commit or digest in each environment, with full adoption across development teams and support for hundreds of services. I went beyond fixing the immediate complaint by putting myself in the customer’s workflow and delivering a durable replacement.

## What to emphasize

- The above-and-beyond action was validating the developer experience yourself before asking them to adopt a new system.
- The home lab was a means to make a better customer decision, not the headline of the story.
- End on the durable customer outcome rather than the effort you personally invested.
