# Tell Me About a Time You Went Above and Beyond for a Customer

**Story:** To choose

## What the interviewer is listening for

- You understand who the customer is and what they actually need.
- You take ownership beyond the narrow boundary of an assigned task.
- You solve the underlying problem rather than doing a one-off favor.

## Skim notes

- Define the customer: external user, developer, partner, or another team.
- Start with their actual pain, urgency, or blocked outcome.
- Show the extra ownership you took and why it mattered.
- Explain how you balanced speed with a durable solution.
- End with the customer outcome and any systemic improvement.

## Details to add before the interview

- What did the customer need, and how did you learn it?
- What was outside your normal scope?
- What tradeoff did you make to help them?
- Did you eliminate the underlying cause for future customers?

## Likely follow-ups

- How did you avoid overcommitting or creating an unsustainable precedent?
- How did you measure customer impact?
- What did you do when the customer’s request conflicted with a broader priority?
