# Tell Me About a Time You Worked Through Ambiguity

**Story:** To choose

## What the interviewer is listening for

- You make progress when requirements, ownership, or the answer is unclear.
- You separate facts, assumptions, and decisions that need validation.
- You create clarity for other people, not only yourself.

## Skim notes

- Choose a problem with unclear scope, owner, requirements, or root cause.
- Define the decision or customer outcome you needed to unblock.
- Explain the first facts you gathered and assumptions you tested.
- Show how you created a plan, owner, experiment, or decision point.
- End with the outcome and the clarity you left behind.

## Details to add before the interview

- What specifically was unknown at the start?
- Which assumptions proved wrong or mattered most?
- How did you decide what to investigate first?
- What mechanism prevented the ambiguity from returning?

## Likely follow-ups

- How did you avoid analysis paralysis?
- When did you involve others?
- What would you do differently with more time?
