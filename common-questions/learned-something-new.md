# Tell Me About a Time You Learned Something New to Solve a Problem

**Leadership Principle:** Learn and Be Curious  
**Use:** [Building a Deliberate Cloud-Native Learning Roadmap](../core-stories/deliberate-cloud-native-learning.md) and [Making CI/CD Easier for Developers](../core-stories/cicd-pipeline-migration.md)

## Draft answer

> As my work expanded into AWS, Kubernetes, and cloud-native platforms, I realized that learning only what an immediate ticket required would limit the problems I could solve. I created a deliberate roadmap around AWS depth, a master’s degree, and the Kubernetes certification path. I use those goals to identify gaps, build hands-on practice, and apply the learning to platform work rather than treating credentials as the outcome. One example was a CI/CD migration: I learned a new delivery technology, recreated a representative environment at home, and built sample projects so I could understand the workflow from a developer’s perspective. That work informed the production design and migration, which gave teams better delivery visibility and was adopted across hundreds of services. The lesson was that structured learning becomes valuable when it changes the quality of a real technical decision.
