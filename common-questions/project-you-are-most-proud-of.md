# What Project Are You Most Proud Of?

**Story:** To choose

## What the interviewer is listening for

- The scale and importance of the outcome.
- Your personal role, decisions, and ownership—not just the team’s achievement.
- Why the work mattered to customers, the business, or the team.
- Whether you can reflect honestly on what made it hard and what you learned.

## Skim notes

- Pick a project with a clear customer or business outcome.
- Set the stakes: what needed to change and why it mattered.
- Be precise about what you personally owned.
- Share the hardest decision, constraint, or turning point.
- Give the result with real evidence.
- Explain why you are proud: impact, growth, team outcome, or durable improvement.

## Details to add before the interview

- What project is the best choice for this question, and why?
- What was the initial problem, scope, and success measure?
- What were your specific responsibilities and key decisions?
- What was difficult, unexpected, or risky?
- What changed as a result, and what evidence proves it?
- What would you do differently now?

## Likely follow-ups

- Why are you more proud of this project than another one?
- What part would have failed without your contribution?
- How did you bring others along or share credit?
