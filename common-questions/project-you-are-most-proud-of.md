# What Project Are You Most Proud Of?

**Leadership Principles:** Think Big; Learn and Be Curious; Ownership; Deliver Results

**Use:** [Pioneering an HGN-Based Impaired-Driving Detection Prototype](../core-stories/hgn-impaired-driving-detection.md)

## Draft answer

> The project I am most proud of is an impaired-driving detection device I helped pioneer during my undergraduate capstone. I had just begun my computer science degree after losing my father to a drunk driver, so the project had personal meaning. I joined a university software-engineering research effort that began with an early sketch for a device that could monitor a driver’s eye-gaze patterns and, when necessary, keep a vehicle from shifting out of neutral.
>
> Although I was early in my degree, I used the project to build the skills it required. I developed an approach based on horizontal gaze nystagmus, incorporated an open-source computer-vision model on an edge device, and designed a small-screen and camera workflow to guide the test. I used AWS for heavier computation and cold storage. I built a working car prototype, then conducted an IRB-approved study and gathered hundreds of ethical samples to evaluate it.
>
> I presented the work at the University of Virginia symposium. The university funded the patent pursuit, I later worked with patent lawyers on the application, and I received the patent earlier this year. I am proud not only of the technical result, but also that the work continues: later student cohorts received grants to keep developing the product.

## What the interviewer is listening for

- The scale and importance of the outcome.
- Your personal role, decisions, and ownership—not just the team’s achievement.
- Why the work mattered to customers, the business, or the team.
- Whether you can reflect honestly on what made it hard and what you learned.

## Skim notes

- Personal mission project.
- Research role opening.

## Details to add before the interview

- What technical and research decisions did you own versus your collaborators?
- What were the study’s exact sample size, evaluation metrics, and results?
- What did the edge device handle versus AWS, and why?
- What details of the patent and vehicle integration are safe to discuss publicly?
- What would you improve in the prototype today?

## Likely follow-ups

- Why was HGN the right basis for the prototype?
- How did you ensure the study and data collection were ethical?
- Which technical problem was the hardest to solve?
- How did you share credit with research collaborators?
