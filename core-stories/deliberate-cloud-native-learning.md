# Building a Deliberate Cloud-Native Learning Roadmap

## Leadership Principle demonstrated

- [Learn and Be Curious](../leadership-principles/learn-and-be-curious.md) — primary

## STAR story

### Situation

As my work increasingly involved AWS, Kubernetes, and cloud-native platforms, I saw that staying effective required more than learning only what an immediate task demanded. The technology and the depth of the problems were continually expanding.

### Task

Build a deliberate learning plan that would strengthen my practical cloud and Kubernetes expertise over time, while continuing to apply that learning in my work.

### Action

I deliberately prioritized three complementary development paths:

- Pursuing the AWS Golden Jacket credential path to build breadth and depth across AWS.
- Completing a master’s degree to strengthen my broader technical foundation and ability to reason through complex systems.
- Aspiring to become a Kubestronaut, using the Kubernetes certification path as a structured way to deepen cloud-native expertise.

Rather than treating each goal as an isolated credential, I used them as a roadmap for identifying knowledge gaps and choosing what to learn next. I connected that learning to the platform and delivery work I was doing, including EKS, CI/CD, and Kubernetes operations.

### Result

I created a sustained, intentional learning practice rather than reacting only to immediate technical demands. The plan continues to build the AWS and Kubernetes knowledge needed to take on more complex platform problems and make better technical decisions.

## Why this story fits

**Learn and Be Curious:** I set ambitious, long-term learning goals, proactively pursued new knowledge, and connected that learning to real technical work rather than waiting for a requirement to force it.
